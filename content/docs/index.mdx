## Need help?

Can't find your answer here? Open a ticket on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/Ab3vYufTh3) with:

- the script name and version,
- your framework (ESX, QBCore, Qbox, QS-Core or standalone),
- the full error from your server console (F8 for client errors).
