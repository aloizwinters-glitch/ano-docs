## Installation

<Steps>
<Step>
Download `ano_example` from [Keymaster](https://keymaster.fivem.net/asset-grants) and place it in your `resources` folder.
</Step>
<Step>
Import `install.sql` into your database.
</Step>
<Step>
Add the item to your inventory (see [Items](#items)).
</Step>
<Step>
Add `ensure ano_example` to your `server.cfg`, after your framework and dependencies.
</Step>
</Steps>
